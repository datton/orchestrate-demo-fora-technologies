Signage

Signs help customers find the entrance, understand the menu, know where to order and pay, and follow safety information. Clear, readable signs with good contrast reduce queues and questions.

Other in-venue items

  • Printed or displayed menus.
  • Seating and table markers.
  • Information about accepted payment methods.
  • Notices about allergens and accessibility.
